⚡ ACT I SPEEDRUN & ROUTING TIPS
• Focus Magic Packs: Magic (blue-name) monsters give ~3.5× XP. Skip white packs unless underleveled.
• Red Vale Weapon Racks: Guaranteed 2nd-tier Martial weapon drop — no muling needed.
• Cemetery Strategy: Slay Draven first, then manually exit & re-enter before Asinia to scale her drops with higher area level.
• Verisium Anvil: Starts with Farrow in Clearfell (Runeseeker quest) — collect 3 Runestones to unlock Armour Runeforging.

⚡ ACT II SPEEDRUN & ROUTING TIPS
• Run Mastodon Badlands BEFORE Keth: Kabala then drops a Lvl 7 skill gem (huge power spike).
• Delay Ascendancy to Lvl 28: Skip the Balbala fight in Traitor's Passage. Instead, farm a backup Djinn Barya from the Watchful Twins in Deshar and pick the Lvl 28 Barya at Trial entry for better end-trial resistances.
• Trial Room Preference: Ritual > Chalice > Hourglass > Gauntlet.
• Target Level: Aim for Lvl 29 for Jamanra; Lvl 30 entering Act III.

⚡ ACT III SPEEDRUN & ROUTING TIPS
• 0.5 Fate of the Vaal Beacons: Activate 6 Vaal Beacons across Act III (Venom Crypts → Infested Barrens → Machine → Sanctum → Waterways) to unlock Vaal Ruins portal.
• 0.5 Runestoning: 3 Runestones in Act 3 (Barrens, Machinarium, Azak Bog) unlock Unique Verisium Runeforging at Mystic Refuge.
• 2nd Ascendancy: Trial of Chaos inside Infested Barrens requires Inscribed Ultimatum (Lvl 34+). Complete before Doryani for 2nd Ascendancy.
• Mektul Flood Timer: Mektul has ~4-min molten-flood timer — overlevel and cap Fire Res.

⚡ ACT IV SPEEDRUN & ROUTING TIPS
• Randomized Island Layout: Weapon Fragments & Matiki are randomized across the four islands (Whakapanu, Shrike, Isle of Kin, Kedge Bay) each league — Kingsmarch dialogue tells you which.
• Free Matiki: Matiki is held captive in a cage on the islands — you MUST click the cage to free her to progress.
• 4 Torn Map Pieces Required: Collect 4 Torn Maps across the 4 islands to open Plunder's Point & unlock 13 Ancient Runes (Runekeeper quest with Farrow).
• Halls of the Dead Tattoo: Pick +5% Fire/Cold/Lightning Resistance for most builds.
• Waterfall Secret: Eye of Hinekora hidden chest behind waterfall drops Spirit Gem Lv12.

⚡ INTERLUDES ROUTING & SPEEDRUN NOTES
• Recommended Order: 5.3 → 5.1 → 5.2. Do 5.3 (Mount Kriar) first for early +40 Spirit power spike, then 5.1 (Ogham), then 5.2 (Khari Bazaar).
• Missable Permanent Rewards: Every reward listed below is permanent and missable — complete all 3 interludes before starting Tier 1 Maps.
• Resistances Drop: Resistances drop the moment you enter an interlude — ensure gear is prepared before zoning in.
• Orbala's 7 Boons (Khari Bazaar): Can be swapped anytime by returning to Orbala's Pillar.
