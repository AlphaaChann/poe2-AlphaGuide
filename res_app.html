Fire Resistance & Fire Pinnacle Bosses

75% FIRE RES TARGET (100%+ OVERCAPPED)

Fire Resistance is a critical target to withstand Atlas pinnacle boss The Arbiter of Ash, Trial of the Sekhemas boss Zarokh, and Expedition boss Olroth.

The Arbiter of Ash

MAIN PINNACLE BOSS
Primary Early Access endgame pinnacle boss in PoE 2. Requires 3 Crisis Fragments from Citadel Guardians. Features lava explosion attacks and degen fields.
Unique Drops: Sacred Flame, Prism of Belief, Morior Invictus

Zarokh, the Temporal

TRIAL OF THE SEKHEMAS
Located at the end of Trial of the Sekhemas. Sand & time-based Fire/Light attacks. Requires high mobility to dodge control attacks.
Unique Drops: Sekhema's Resolve, Temporalis, Sandstorm Visage

Olroth, Origin of the Fall

EXPEDITION ABERRATION
Encountered inside Expedition Logbooks. Physical & Fire attacks with shield block bypass mechanics (must break Olroth's shield).
Unique Drops: Svalinn Shield, Olroth's Resolve, Shattered Triskelion

Act III: Infernal Highlands Target

CAMPAIGN PROGRESSION
Target 75% Fire Res before entering lava zones and Zarokh's Temple of Chaos.
Target: 75% Fire Res

Lightning Resistance & Lightning Pinnacle Bosses

75% LIGHTNING RES TARGET (100%+ OVERCAPPED)

Lightning Resistance is mandatory to survive Doryani, Jamanra Abomination, and Breach Realm pinnacle boss Xesht.

Doryani, Royal Thaumaturge

STONE CITADEL GUARDIAN
Guardian of Stone Citadel. Uses high-voltage Lightning & deadly Chaos element combinations.
Unique Drops: Stone Citadel Key, High Tier Uniques

Jamanra, the Abomination

COPPER CITADEL GUARDIAN
Guardian of Copper Citadel in Vastiri Desert. Massive Lightning shock & Physical attacks.
Unique Drops: Copper Citadel Key, Abomination Gear

Xesht, We That Are One

BREACH REALM PINNACLE
Accessed by collecting Breach Splinters. Fast Lightning & Chaos attacks that cage players.
Unique Drops: Choir of the Storm, The Pandemonius, Xoph's Blood

Act II: Vastiri Desert Target

CAMPAIGN PROGRESSION
Mandatory 75% Lightning Res target to mitigate sandstorm lightning bursts in Vastiri Desert.
Target: 75% Lightning Res

Cold Resistance & Cold Pinnacle Bosses

75% COLD RES TARGET (100%+ OVERCAPPED)

Cold Resistance is essential for Ritual boss The King in the Mists, Geonor (Iron Count), and Freythorn boss fights.

The Iron Count / Geonor

IRON CITADEL GUARDIAN
Guardian of Iron Citadel. Physical ice cleaves and wide Cold freezing areas.
Unique Drops: Iron Citadel Key, Geonor's Armor

The King in the Mists

RITUAL ENDGAME BOSS
Accessed via Ritual Tribute tickets. Spawns Cold degeneration mist that melts life without high cold resistance.
Unique Drops: The Burden of Shadows, Ingenuity, Pragmatism

Act I: Freythorn Target

CAMPAIGN PROGRESSION
75% Cold Res target in Act I prior to fighting King in the Mist in Freythorn.
Target: 75% Cold Res

Chaos Resistance & Endgame Trials

+30% TO 75% CHAOS RES TARGET (MANDATORY ENDGAME)

During the campaign, Chaos Resistance can be partially bypassed, but in Endgame Atlas (specifically Trialmaster, Simulacrum, and Doryani/Xesht), Chaos Resistance +30% to 75% is MANDATORY to prevent one-shots.

The Trialmaster

TRIAL OF CHAOS / ULTIMATUM
Complete 10 rounds of Trial of Chaos using Bahlak Key. Chaos poison attacks & player-chosen debuff modifiers.
Unique Drops: Hateforge, Mahuxotl's Machination, The Adorned

Simulacrum Bosses (Omniphobia & Kosis)

DELIRIUM WAVE 15
Survive 15 Delirium waves (300 Simulacrum Splinters). Chaos damage & Action Speed slow debuffs.
Unique Drops: Delirium Cluster Jewels, Unique Amulets

Doryani & Xesht Dual Damage

CITADEL & BREACH
Both bosses deal simultaneous Lightning + Chaos damage.
Requirement: High Chaos + Lightning Res

Overcapped Resistance & Spell Suppression Rules

3 CORE ENDGAME SURVIVAL RULES

1. Overcapped Resistance (100%+)

ANTI-CURSE & PENETRATION
In addition to standard 75% cap, ensure total resistance exceeds 100%+ (Overcap) because Pinnacle bosses forcibly inflict Curses or Elemental Penetration.

2. Minimum +30% Chaos Res

ENDGAME SURVIVAL
Without Chaos Res in endgame, Trialmaster and Simulacrum degen attacks will instantly one-shot you.

3. 100% Spell Suppression / Armour

SPELL NUKES DEFENSE
Almost all Pinnacle boss nuke attacks are Spell-based. Achieving 100% Spell Suppression permanently reduces boss spell damage by 50%.
