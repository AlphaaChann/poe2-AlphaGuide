I

The Basics & Trading

💡 THE FOUNDATION OF POE2 ECONOMY In Path of Exile 2, currency items serve a dual purpose: they are direct crafting materials for items AND the global medium of trade exchange. Understanding when to spend vs when to sell is the core of wealth generation.

1. Gold vs Trade Currency

Gold drops directly from monsters and chests in PoE2. Gold cannot be traded directly between players, but it is required to pay listing fees at the Currency Exchange (Ange in Kingsmarch) and vendor items.

  • Exalted Orbs & Divine Orbs: The primary high-tier trade standard currencies for buying gear, uniques, and high-value bases.
  • Chaos Orbs & Alchemy Orbs: Mid-tier standard currencies for map rolling and mid-level item trades.
  • Gold Management: Always maintain at least 50k–100k Gold so you never run out of currency exchange fee capacity during mapping.

2. Trading Mindset

Currency generation is about liquid volume and minimizing hideout downtime. Wealth accumulates exponentially when items move out of your inventory quickly rather than sitting in tabs waiting for over-priced buyers.

II

Loot Filters Setup

⚠️ HIDE THE CLUTTER Picking up 1-gold vendor magic items in Tier 10 maps loses you up to 70% of your potential Divine Orbs per hour!

1. Strictness Escalation

As your character strength grows, increase your loot filter strictness:

  • Campaign (Regular / Semi-Strict): Show gear upgrades, sockets, gold, and all currency types.
  • Early Mapping (Strict): Hide low-level base items, white items without sockets, and sub-tier 1 flask bases.
  • Endgame T15+ (Very Strict / Strict+): Show ONLY high-tier currency, high-value bases (ilvl 82+), valuable runes, catalysts, omens, and tier 1 uniques.

2. Customizing Filters for Profit

Highlight high-demand league items such as Uncut Gems (Lv19+), Barya Djinn Keys, Catalysts, and Waystones (T14+) with bright distinct colors and audio drop alerts.

IV

Dump Tabbing & Pricing

📦 DUMP TAB METHODOLOGY Do not price individual 5-chaos rares after every map. Dump everything into a designated quad tab and keep mapping!

Step-by-Step Dump Tab Process:

  1. Set Quad Tab to Fixed Price: Set an entire Quad Dump Tab to 1 Exalted Orb (or 15 Chaos).
  2. Fill the Tab: Dump all unidentified/identified rare items into the tab after each map.
  3. Lower Price Daily: If items don't sell after 1 day, lower the tab price to 10 Chaos → 5 Chaos → Vendor remainder for Gold/Reforging.
  4. Instant Whispers Warning: If you receive 5 whispers within 10 seconds of dumping an item, you mispriced a high-tier item! Take it out and price check on Trade.
V

Mapping & Time Efficiency

⏱️ THE "HIDEOUT IS LAVA" RULE Every minute spent standing still in your hideout is 0 Exalted Orbs earned.

1. Map Preparation in Batches

Never roll one map at a time. Roll 10 to 20 Waystones at once using Transmutations/Alchemies, check for dangerous mods (e.g. No Mana Regen, Reflect), store them in a Map Bag, and run them sequentially.

2. Inventory & Portal Hygiene

  • Keep Portal Scrolls / Uncut Gems in fixed corners of your inventory.
  • Only return to hideout when inventory is 100% full.
  • Use Ctrl + Right Click for instant stash transfers.
VI

Farming Mechanics

Top Currency Farming Strategies

STRATEGY 1

Essence & Omen Rushing

Spec your Atlas nodes into Essence duplication and Omen drop chance. Run fast linear maps, burst Essence frozen packs, and exit.

Requirements: Fast movement skill + high single-target DPS.
STRATEGY 2

Expedition Logbook Farming

Farm Dannig and Tujen logbooks. Tujen gambles yield immense stacks of Chaos, Exalted, and Divine Orbs.

Requirements: High Area Damage + Explosive Immunity management.
STRATEGY 3

High Tier Waystone Boss Rushing

Run T15/T16 maps straight to the boss for High Tier Waystone drops and Pinnacle Boss Key fragments.

Requirements: Boss burst DPS + Movement speed.
VII

High Value Crafting Bases

🛡️ HIGH ITEM LEVEL BASES = BIG GOLD & TRADE High Item Level (ilvl 82+) white bases with maximum sockets sell for high trade value to crafters.

Bases to Always Filter & Check:

  • ilvl 82+ Science Scale & Vaal Regalia Bases: Top energy shield & hybrid crafting armours.
  • ilvl 82+ Gemini Bows & Rattling Sceptres: Premier attack & caster weapon bases.
  • 5-Socket & 6-Socket Weapons/Body Armours: High value for socketing Runes of Aldur or vendor salvaging.
VIII

Extra Currency Methods

Alternative Wealth Generation Methods

  • Salvage Bench Crafting: Salvage 5-socket armours at the Smithing bench to recover raw Verisium & High Runes.
  • Early League Vendor Flipping: Buy uncut gems from town vendors early in league launch and sell desired meta gems on Trade.
  • Boss Carrying: Sell pinnacle boss kills (Tavakai, Doryani, Sekhema) to players needing campaign/ascendancy completion.
IX

Asynchronous Trade (Ange)

⚖️ CURRENCY EXCHANGE (ANGE IN KINGSMARCH) Trade currency, omens, essences, and fragments instantly without whispering or waiting for players!

How to Maximize Asynchronous Trade:

  1. Talk to Ange in Kingsmarch (unlocked in Act 4).
  2. Place your items (e.g. 50 Essences) into the seller box and select your desired currency output (e.g. Divine Orbs).
  3. Set competitive market prices by checking the live ratio tab.
  4. Collect your profits directly from Ange whenever you return from mapping!
